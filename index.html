Online Application for Leave

CS Form No. 6, Revised 2020 — employee information and Sections 1–6 are completed online.

CS Form No. 6
Applicant Information
6. Details of Application
Working days are suggested from the date range with Saturdays and Sundays excluded. Review the value before submitting because holidays, school calendars, and other non-working days may require adjustment.

Online Leave Workflow

  1. Employee completes the same information required in CS Form No. 6.
  2. SAMS stores the application and generates the printable form.
  3. HR/Admin records leave-credit certification in Section 7.A.
  4. School Head records the recommendation in Section 7.B.
  5. When the approving authority's action is received, HR/Admin records Section 7.C / 7.D and the final status.
Teaching and non-teaching personnel: both may use this same online application. Leave-credit computation and entitlement rules remain separate from the form itself and can be connected to the Leave Ledger module later.

Employee Masterlist

Personnel may be listed here even without a SAMS login account.

Tip: Use Sync SAMS Users to add existing registered user profiles to the masterlist automatically without duplicating matching emails.

How the Masterlist is Used

  1. Administrator/HR maintains employee names.
  2. Supply Officer selects a walk-in employee from the list.
  3. Office and position are filled automatically when available.
  4. The request is recorded as Walk-In in the same Supplies/RIS monitoring table.
  5. If the email matches a SAMS account, the transaction is linked to that user automatically.
